Session 10 · Giving & Receiving

The Language of Your Love

How you give, how you receive, and what love feels like when it’s truly working

God made you to give and receive love in particular ways. Not just any love — the specific kind that reaches you, fills you, and flows most naturally from you.

This inventory explores three dimensions of your love life: how you tend to express love to others, how you most deeply receive it, and what love feels like for you when it is truly working. These three may surprise you — they don’t always match.

Answer as honestly as you can. There are no right answers. Every language is a gift.

A Prayer Before Beginning

Lord, You made me for love — to give it and to receive it. You who are Love itself have poured that love into me and shaped me to carry it in particular ways.

As I move through this inventory, open my eyes to see how You wired me. Let me be honest about what fills me and what flows from me. And let what I discover here draw me closer to You and to the people You have placed in my life.

Amen.
