Step Three · Activity
The Prayer Cord

You did not find your way here by accident. Ten women’s yeses — ten moments of grace — carried you to this threshold. Like a Marian decade, you’ll name each one as you tie a knot in a red cord you’ll wear for the next fifteen weeks.

What you’ll need: a 24-inch length of red six-strand cotton embroidery floss (DMC #321 is perfect), or the optional Prayer Cord Kit if you ordered one.

Tie ten knots, spaced evenly. At each knot, name a woman whose yes made yours possible. A mother, a grandmother, a friend, a saint — any woman whose life made yours. Wear the cord for the next fifteen weeks as a quiet prayer. At Session 15, we’ll cut it off together.
