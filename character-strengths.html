The Framework

What Are Character Strengths?

The VIA Classification of Character Strengths is the result of a 3-year project by 55 scientists who studied virtues across cultures, religions, and philosophies to identify the core strengths that make humans flourish.

They discovered 24 character strengths organized under 6 universal virtues. Everyone possesses all 24 strengths to varying degrees, but your "signature strengths" are the ones that feel most essential to who you are.

Research shows that using your signature strengths leads to greater well-being, engagement, meaning, and accomplishment. When you operate from your strengths, you're living as you were designed to live.

"As each has received a gift, use it to serve one another, as good stewards of God's varied grace." — 1 Peter 4:10

The 24 Strengths

Organized by Six Virtues

📚

Wisdom

Cognitive strengths that involve acquiring and using knowledge

💡

Creativity

Thinking of novel and productive ways to do things. You see unique solutions and express ideas in original ways.

🔍

Curiosity

Taking an interest in experience for its own sake. You find subjects and topics fascinating and love to explore and discover.

⚖️

Judgment

Thinking things through and examining them from all sides. You weigh evidence fairly and can change your mind when warranted.

📖

Love of Learning

Mastering new skills and topics. You love the process of learning, whether in a class or on your own.

🦉

Perspective

Being able to provide wise counsel to others. You see the big picture and help others make sense of the world.

🦁

Courage

Emotional strengths that involve exercising will to accomplish goals despite opposition

⚔️

Bravery

Not shrinking from threat, challenge, or pain. You act on convictions even when unpopular and speak up for what's right.

🏃

Perseverance

Finishing what you start. You persist despite obstacles and take pleasure in completing tasks.

🎯

Honesty

Speaking the truth and presenting yourself genuinely. You are authentic and take responsibility for your actions.

⚡

Zest

Approaching life with excitement and energy. You feel alive and activated, and you bring enthusiasm to what you do.

💕

Humanity

Interpersonal strengths that involve caring for and befriending others

❤️

Love

Valuing close relationships with others. You have deep, genuine connections where sharing and caring go both ways.

🤲

Kindness

Doing favors and good deeds for others. You enjoy helping people, even those you don't know well.

🧠

Social Intelligence

Being aware of the motives and feelings of others. You know what makes people tick and how to fit into different situations.

⚖️

Justice

Civic strengths that underlie healthy community life

🤝

Teamwork

Working well as a member of a group. You're loyal to the team, do your share, and work for the group's success.

⚖️

Fairness

Treating all people the same according to notions of fairness and justice. You don't let personal feelings bias your decisions.

👑

Leadership

Organizing group activities and seeing that they happen. You maintain good relations while encouraging the group to get things done.

🧘

Temperance

Strengths that protect against excess

🕊️

Forgiveness

Forgiving those who have done wrong. You give people second chances and let go of resentment.

🙏

Humility

Letting your accomplishments speak for themselves. You don't seek the spotlight and see yourself accurately.

🛡️

Prudence

Being careful about your choices. You don't say or do things you might regret and think through consequences.

⚓

Self-Regulation

Regulating what you feel and do. You're disciplined and in control of your appetites and emotions.

✨

Transcendence

Strengths that forge connections to the larger universe and provide meaning

🌸

Appreciation of Beauty

Noticing and appreciating beauty, excellence, and skill in all domains. You experience awe and wonder at the world.

🙏

Gratitude

Being aware of and thankful for good things. You take time to express thanks and don't take blessings for granted.

🌟

Hope

Expecting the best and working to achieve it. You believe the future can be good and that you can influence it.

😄

Humor

Liking to laugh and bring smiles to others. You see the light side of situations and use humor to connect.

🕯️

Spirituality

Having coherent beliefs about the higher purpose and meaning of life. Your beliefs shape your actions and provide comfort.

Putting It to Work

Living from Your Strengths

Your signature strengths (typically your top 5-7) are the ones that feel most authentically you. Using them energizes rather than drains you. They're often so natural you might not even recognize them as strengths.

Research shows that finding new ways to use your signature strengths leads to increased happiness and decreased depression—effects that last for months. This isn't self-help fluff; it's rigorous positive psychology.

In A Symphony of Grace, we explore your strengths as entrusted gifts— given by God for your flourishing and to bless others. When you live from your strengths, you're living as you were designed.

"For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared beforehand, that we should walk in them."

— Ephesians 2:10
