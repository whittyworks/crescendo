The Concept

What is a Rule of Life?

A Rule of Life is a rhythmic pattern of practices that order your days around what matters most. It's not a rigid schedule or a to-do list—it's a loving structure that helps you say yes to the life you're called to live, and a gentle no to the things that pull you away from it.

For centuries, religious communities have used Rules of Life to create sacred rhythm. But this practice isn't just for monks and nuns—it's for any woman who wants to live with intention, presence, and peace.

Think of it as the trellis that supports a growing vine. The trellis doesn't force the vine to grow—it simply provides the structure that allows it to flourish.

Your Rule of Life does the same for your soul. It creates space for what matters while protecting you from the tyranny of the urgent.

The Framework

The Five Movements

Just as a symphony is composed of movements that work together to create something beautiful, your Rule of Life flows through five essential areas. Each one is necessary. None stands alone.

Movement One
🕊️

Adoration

Prayer, worship, and time with God

Before anything else, you are a beloved daughter of God. This movement cultivates that relationship through prayer, worship, and sacred encounter.

Movement Two
✨

Restoration

Rest, self-care, health, and renewal

You cannot pour from an empty cup. This movement honors your need for rest, beauty, and care of the body and soul God entrusted to you.

Movement Three
💕

Relation

Marriage, friendships, and community

We are made for communion. This movement tends to the relationships that sustain us—your spouse, friendships, and faith community.

Movement Four
🏡

Vocation

Motherhood, family, and home

Your home is your domestic church. This movement embraces the beautiful, demanding work of raising children and creating a home where love dwells.

Movement Five
🌿

Mission

Work, service, and calling in the world

You have gifts meant to bless beyond your home. This movement honors your work and the ways you're called to contribute to God's kingdom.
