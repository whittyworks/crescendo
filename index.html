The Heart of Crescendo

Beholding Your God-Given Design

You are not an accident. You are not a mistake. Every detail of who you are—your body, your mind, your spirit—was designed with intention by a God who delights in you.

My Holy Crescendo is an invitation to see yourself through His eyes. Through Scripture, the wisdom of saints, and gentle practices of self-discovery, you'll uncover the beauty that's been there all along.
