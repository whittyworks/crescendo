The Journey

What is A Symphony of Grace?

A Symphony of Grace is a 15-week immersive program designed to help you understand the unique, once-in-a-universe symphony that God created you to be. Together we explore the fullness of who you are — body, mind, and spirit — through Scripture, the wisdom of the saints, and carefully chosen frameworks that illuminate God's design in you.

The retreat gave you a taste. The program gives you the whole table.

Four Movements

The Modules of Your Symphony

Like the movements of a great symphony, each module builds upon the last — creating a complete, coherent composition of grace, beauty, and self-understanding.

Movement I

Foundation

Weeks 1–3 · Rooted in Christ

  • Daughter of the King — Chosen before time, called as His own
  • Named by the Father — The intimate name He alone can speak over you
  • Receiving His Love — Learning to truly receive the love of the Father

Movement II

Body

Weeks 4–6 · Fearfully & Wonderfully Made

  • Colors of Creation — The unique palette God used to paint you
  • Sacred Style Essence — How the Lord is glorified in your presence
  • Sacred Silhouettes — The beauty and dignity of your created form

Movement III

Mind

Weeks 7–10 · Woven in Wisdom

  • Four Temperament Blend — Your God-given way of being in the world
  • 16 Personality Types — Your way of seeing and engaging the world
  • Gifts of Grace — Graces given to you alone, meant to be poured out
  • Love Languages — How you give and receive love

Movement IV

Spirit

Weeks 11–15 · Alive in Grace

  • Light in the Shadows — Wound work; healing in the light of Christ
  • 42 Virtues — Growing in holiness with St. Albert the Great
  • 25 Charisms — Finding your spiritual gifts to further the kingdom
  • Rule of Life — Ordering your life rightly, with God at the center
  • Beautiful Symphony — A celebration of the unique masterpiece you are
