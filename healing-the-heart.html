Healing the Heart

A Workbook for Inner Restoration

Sometimes the journey toward wholeness requires tending to the places within us that still ache. This workbook is being crafted with prayer and care to guide you through a gentle process of healing, surrender, and restoration.

Something Beautiful Is Coming

Healing the Heart is a guided workbook designed to help you bring the broken, wounded, and weary places of your heart to the One who heals.

Through Scripture, reflection, and gentle exercises, you'll be invited to acknowledge what hurts, release what you've been carrying, and receive the restoration only God can give.
