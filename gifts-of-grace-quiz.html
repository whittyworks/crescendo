Gifts of Grace

A prayerful inventory of how God wired you

God did not make you in generalities. He made you in particulars — with a specific mind, a specific heart, a specific way of moving through the world. These are not accidents. They are gifts.

This inventory will help you identify your 24 Gifts of Grace — the natural strengths built into the particular woman God made you to be. Read each statement and rate how true it feels, not how you wish it were or how it was at your best, but how it is for you, most of the time.

There are no right answers. Every gift is needed in the Body of Christ.

A Prayer Before Beginning

Lord, You who knit me together in my mother’s womb — You did not make me by accident. Every particular of who I am was made by You and for You.

As I move through this inventory, open my eyes to see myself as You see me. Let me receive what is true, without inflating it or dismissing it. Let me be honest about who I am, knowing that who I am is Your handiwork.

May what I discover here draw me closer to You and help me live more fully as the woman You made me to be.

Amen.
