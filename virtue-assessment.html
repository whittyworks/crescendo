Welcome to this transformative assessment based on the 42 virtues described in The Paradise of the Soul by St. Albert the Great (Albertus Magnus), the great 13th-century Doctor of the Church.

What to expect:

  • 126 thoughtful questions exploring each of the 42 classical Christian virtues
  • Simple agree/disagree format—no right or wrong answers
  • Approximately 20-30 minutes to complete
  • Receive your personalized top 10 virtues with descriptions
  • Option to view all 42 virtues ranked

How to approach this: Answer honestly based on your current life, not an idealized version of yourself. This is a tool for self-reflection and spiritual growth, not judgment. The more truthful you are, the more valuable your results will be.
