Every baptized Christian has charisms. The question isn't if the Holy Spirit has gifted you — it's which gifts He has placed within you for the building up of His Body.

This quiz explores all 25 charisms through two reflective statements each. Rate how true each statement feels in your life right now — there are no right or wrong answers, only honest ones.

You'll receive your top charisms with descriptions to begin your discernment journey.

A Prayer Before We Begin

In the Name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit.

Holy Spirit, I praise you because you are holy,
and I thank you for dwelling in my heart and working in my life.
Make me more aware of your presence
so I can closely follow the path you call me to walk.

Help me to see you more clearly;
to recognize your animating movement in my life.
Inspire and empower me to be your tabernacle,
a home for the indwelling love of God —

and give me clarity as I discern your will
through this inventory.

Amen.

A Word on Discernment

This quiz is a starting point — a helpful mirror to begin noticing where the Holy Spirit may be at work in you. Your top scores indicate charisms worth exploring more deeply.

True charism discernment involves prayer, experimentation, and community confirmation. Ask: Do I feel joy and energy here? Do others benefit? Does my service bear fruit?

You likely have 2–4 strong charisms. Don't be surprised if several rise to the surface — the Spirit is generous!
