A Guided Prayer Experience

Name Tag from Heaven

"I have called you by name" — Isaiah 43:1

🕊️

Settle Into Stillness

Take a quiet moment to breathe and invite Jesus into this space.

Breathe

Breathe slowly with the circle. In... and out...

"Before I formed you in the womb I knew you, before you were born I dedicated you." — Jeremiah 1:5
👑

Listen for Your Name

Close your eyes for a moment. Ask Jesus:

"Jesus, what do You call me?"

Listen for the word or phrase He places on your heart — Beloved, Daughter, Brave, Light-Bearer, Chosen, Worthy...

💔

Surface the Lie

What is one lie you've believed about yourself? A whisper that has told you that you are less than who God made you to be?

✨

Receive the Truth

Beneath the lie, write the truth that replaces it. What does God say instead?

If you're unsure, simply start with: "I am... "

📖

Anchor in Scripture

Choose a Scripture that speaks to your heart:

Identity & Belonging
"I have called you by name; you are mine."
— Isaiah 43:1
Unconditional Love
"I have loved you with an everlasting love; I have drawn you with unfailing kindness."
— Jeremiah 31:3
Enough in Him
"My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness."
— 2 Corinthians 12:9
Never Alone
"I will never leave you nor forsake you."
— Hebrews 13:5
Freedom from the Past
"Behold, I am making all things new."
— Revelation 21:5
Rest & Acceptance
"Come to me, all who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest."
— Matthew 11:28
