Your Way of Seeing

16 Personality Types & Cognitive Functions

✦   ✦   ✦

God did not wire every mind the same. The way you take in the world, process meaning, make decisions, and move through life — these are not random. They are written into you by a God who creates with intention. This guided reflection will help you discover your way of seeing.

Rather than a traditional quiz, this is a guided conversation. You'll be presented with real-life scenarios and asked to respond honestly — not to pick the "right" answer, but to describe what actually happens in you.

There are no wrong answers. The more honestly and specifically you respond, the more accurate your result will be. Answer with your first instinct. Don't edit yourself.

This reflection takes about 10–15 minutes. Find a quiet moment before you begin.
