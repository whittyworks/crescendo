About This Assessment

This assessment uses scenario-based questions designed to reveal your natural temperament without leading you toward any particular answer. Unlike tests with obvious questions like "Do you enjoy parties?", these scenarios explore how you actually respond in various situations.

This test recognizes ALL temperament blends—including combinations that some frameworks consider "impossible" (like sanguine-melancholic or choleric-phlegmatic). Your results will show percentages across all four temperaments.

  • 40 questions across 8 dimensions of temperament
  • 10-15 minutes to complete
  • Percentage results for all four temperaments
  • No "right" answers—just honest responses

Tip: Answer based on your first instinct and how you typically behave, not how you think you should behave or how you act in exceptional circumstances.
