What Makes This Different

The Personal Journey offers everything from A Symphony of Grace—but tailored entirely to you. No group schedule. No shared pacing. Just deep, focused work on your unique design.

👤

Individual Attention

Every session is just you and me. No sharing time with others. Your questions, your struggles, your breakthroughs—all given the space they deserve.

🎨

Fully Customized

We move at your pace, focus on what matters most to you, and adapt the journey to your life season. This isn't a program you fit into—it fits you.

📅

Flexible Scheduling

Meet weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly—whatever rhythm serves you best. Sessions scheduled around your life, not a fixed cohort calendar.

🔍

Deeper Dive

With individual time, we go deeper into your color analysis, personality, strengths, shadow work, and spiritual practices than group settings allow.

💬

Ongoing Support

Between sessions, you have access to me via email for questions, insights, and encouragement. You're not alone on this journey.

🌱

Extended Journey

While the cohort is 15 weeks, your personal journey can extend as long as you need—16, 20, 24 weeks or more. We finish when you're ready.

Is This For You?

The Personal Journey is Perfect If...

  • You crave individual attention and want a coach's full focus on your unique journey
  • Group settings feel overwhelming or you process best one-on-one
  • You want to go deeper than a cohort format allows
  • Your schedule doesn't align with cohort dates, but you don't want to wait
  • You're in a tender season (grief, transition, healing) that needs extra care
  • You've done therapy/spiritual direction and want something that integrates body, mind, and spirit
  • You're ready to invest significantly in yourself and this transformative work
  • You want complete privacy—no sharing your story with a group
  • You need flexibility in pacing due to life circumstances
  • You want accountability and support between sessions

A Note on Readiness

The Personal Journey is a significant investment—of time, energy, and resources. This work is for women who are ready to show up fully, do the inner work, and commit to their own transformation. If that's you, I'm honored to walk alongside you.

Common Questions

What You're Wondering

How is this different from the 15-week cohort?

The cohort is group-based with a set schedule and community focus. The Personal Journey is one-on-one, fully customized, and scheduled around your life. You get my complete attention, deeper exploration, and the flexibility to move at your own pace.

How long does the Personal Journey take?

Most clients complete the journey in 4-6 months with 15-24 sessions. However, we can extend or condense based on your needs and preferences. Some women prefer weekly sessions and finish in 4 months; others meet monthly and take 8-10 months.

Where do we meet?

All sessions are conducted via Zoom for maximum flexibility. You can be anywhere in the world and still work with me one-on-one.

What if I need to reschedule sessions?

Life happens! You can reschedule sessions with 24-hour notice. I build in flexibility because I know you're juggling many things.

Do I need to be Catholic?

All Christian women are welcome! The journey draws from Catholic tradition and saints, but the core principles of understanding your God-given design apply across denominational lines.

What if I've already done personality tests?

Perfect! We'll build on what you know. The Personal Journey integrates all these pieces (personality, body type, colors, strengths) into a cohesive understanding of who God made you to be. Previous work enriches the journey.

Can I try the Prelude first?

Absolutely! I recommend starting with the free 3-day Prelude to get a taste of the Crescendo approach. It will help you discern if the Personal Journey is right for you.

Are payment plans available?

Yes! I offer flexible payment options to make this investment manageable. We'll discuss what works best for you during our discovery call.

What if I'm in therapy?

The Personal Journey complements therapy beautifully. This is not therapy or mental health treatment—it's spiritual formation and self-discovery. Many clients work with both a therapist and me simultaneously.
