The Framework

Two Lenses on Love

The 5 Love Languages, developed by Dr. Gary Chapman, describe the primary ways we express and prefer to receive love. When we speak someone's love language, they feel truly seen and cherished.

The 7 Love Styles describe different dimensions of how we connect in relationships—from intellectual and emotional to physical and practical. Understanding your style helps you recognize what you naturally give and what you deeply need.

Together, these frameworks reveal the how of your heart—how you naturally give love, how you long to receive it, and the dimensions that matter most to you.

"Beloved, let us love one another, for love is from God, and whoever loves has been born of God and knows God." — 1 John 4:7

Putting It Together

Love as a Pathway to Healing

Your love language shows what makes you feel loved. Your love style shows the dimensions through which you most naturally connect. Together, they reveal the landscape of your heart.

Understanding these patterns isn't about labeling yourself—it's about self-awareness and compassion. When you know how you naturally give and receive love, you can communicate your needs more clearly and love others more intentionally.

In A Symphony of Grace, we explore how God speaks every love language perfectly—and how understanding your unique love style helps you receive His love more fully and share it more freely with others.
